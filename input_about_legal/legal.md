## Privacy

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, embeds no third-party scripts and
makes no network requests of its own. It does not collect personal data, because
there is nothing here that could. A copy served over the web will produce ordinary
access logs at whatever host it sits on; that host's privacy policy governs those,
not this page.
