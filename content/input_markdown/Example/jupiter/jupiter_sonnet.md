Jupiter is the largest planet in the solar system, with a mass roughly two and a
half times that of all the other planets combined. It has no solid surface; the
atmosphere thickens with depth until hydrogen becomes metallic.

Its four largest moons — Io, Europa, Ganymede and Callisto — were the first
objects observed orbiting something other than Earth, and were the observation
that made the geocentric model untenable.
